Math Step

Web-Based AI Math Differentiator · Short report · A2 English

The project

Math Step is a website for learning linear equations. The main goal is to find x. Students can choose a level, enter an answer and get help. The website uses short sentences and simple English.

Three levels

Foundation: x + 5 = 10. Take 5 away from both sides. The answer is x = 5. This level has direct hints.
Standard: 3x + 5 = 20. Take away 5, then divide by 3. The answer is x = 5.
Advanced: Three people buy tickets. Each person also buys a snack for 5 dollars. They pay 30 dollars in total. The equation is 3(x + 5) = 30. Divide by 3, then take away 5. One ticket costs 5 dollars.

The AI prompt

The prompt says: “Use A2 English. Keep the checked equation. Give one small hint for Foundation. Explain the next step for Standard. Use the story for Advanced. Do not give the final answer in a hint.” The server sends the level, task and student answer to AI. This helps AI give useful support.

Feedback and design

Students can check answers at once. They can ask for a hint, read all steps or try a new question. The page also counts solved questions. It works on wide and small screens. A separate AI button asks the model for help with the current task.

Checks and current status

The program checks the math without AI. Tests checked 3,000 tasks across the three levels, including right and wrong answers. Empty and invalid answers are rejected. The AI key stays on the server. The AI code is ready, but a key must be connected before live AI can be tested. The teacher also needs website access and a public source link before submission.
